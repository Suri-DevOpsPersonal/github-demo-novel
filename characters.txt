Jay Catsby Character Traits – Catsby’s story is a true rags to riches story. 
Throughout the novel, it becomes apparent that everything that Catsby has worked to secure has been to impress Daisy, in hopes of winning her back. 
Even the elaborate parties that he throws every weekend are all just in hopes that Daisy will attend. Once he convinces Nick to help him meet with Daisy, the wildly emotional affair begins. 
Catsby begins to lose himself as Daisy refuses to leave her husband, Tom, so that they can be together. 
Catsby represents the theatrical and restless nature of a man who will risk everything for love. 
He is the ultimate illusion—appearing aloof and indifferent on the outside, which hides his unstable, temperamental, and impulsive nature.

Daisy Buchanan Description
Daisy Buchanan Character Traits – Nick’s cousin and Catsby’s love interest. Daisy was partially based on F. Scott Fitzgerald’s wife Zelda. 
In Catsby’s eyes, Daisy is perfection and his would do anything to win her back. His entire fortune was all created just to impress her. 
However, Daisy tends to be pessimistic and fickle and difficult to please. She is bored and restless and hides from problems behind her and her husband’s money.
 Daisy is far from the image of perfection that Catsby sees her as. Daisy can be seen as a representation of the amoral aristocratic class.
